Some entries of curation.json whose evidence.review is "upstream_community" are derived from
the OpenNeuro annotations published by the Neurobagel project:

  https://github.com/neurobagel/openneuro-annotations
  commit 116676db7114b68338c48df2d6bb804c99e8c354

Each such entry names the upstream file it comes from in its evidence.source.
What is taken is the annotation tool's Annotations block of a column (which variable the column
is about, which term each value means, which values are missing).
It is rewritten onto the pinned Neurobagel vocabulary and checked against NEMAR's own copy of the
dataset's participants.tsv; nothing is copied that does not fit that table.
The upstream files used for the tests are kept, unchanged, under test/neurobagel/upstream/.
