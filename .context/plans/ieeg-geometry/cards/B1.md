## Goal

`ieeg_geometry.freesurfer` computes template-space positions for subjects that ship a FreeSurfer subject directory, verifies them, and produces `space-fsaverage` or `space-MNI305` electrode files, covering on003848, on004194, on004473 and on005415.

## Context

- The plan's "Lead rulings" section (evidence grades, the sEEG plausibility rule, the c_ras choice, open references, one-hemisphere surfaces) overrides this card wherever they differ; use `Fit`'s evidence grade, never a raw threshold of your own.
- Read the plan, `census.md` (track B), the L1 package. FreeSurfer facts you need: surface coordinates are tkrRAS; scanner RAS = tkrRAS + c_ras (from the surface footer, read by L1); `mri/transforms/talairach.xfm` maps the subject's scanner RAS to MNI305; `surf/?h.sphere.reg` registers each subject vertex to the fsaverage sphere.
- Fixtures: cut-down real files from on003848 (6 subjects, verified needing c_ras) and on004194 (NYU subjects as declared, UMCU subjects with c_ras); data from `https://data.nemar.org/<id>/<version>/<path>`.

## Scope

`scripts/ieeg_geometry/ieeg_geometry/freesurfer.py`, the fsaverage6 `sphere.reg` (or `sphere`) surfaces added to the vendored references with the same license files, tests and fixtures; nothing else.

## Decided design

Settled:
- Frame of the electrodes relative to the subject: decided by L1's `fit_with_offset` against the subject's own pial surfaces: as declared means tkrRAS, with c_ras means scanner RAS; if neither passes, the subject is refused with the distances.
- Surface-based method (preferred) for a contact within 3.0 mm of the subject's pial surface when `sphere.reg` exists: nearest subject pial vertex, its `sphere.reg` position, the nearest fsaverage6 sphere vertex, that vertex's fsaverage6 pial position; label `fsaverage`.
- Affine method for every other contact (depth contacts, or no `sphere.reg`): scanner RAS through `talairach.xfm`; label `MNI305`.
- One electrodes file per label: contacts mapped by the surface method go to `space-fsaverage`, the rest to `space-MNI305`, unless the subject has no surface-mapped contacts. Both are verified with L1 against fsaverage (the surface method must pass the ECoG rule; the affine set must pass the sEEG rule); a failing set is refused and reported, never written.
- Sidecars as in card C1 (provenance text names the method per file, the inputs, the distances, and says the affine method is approximate). Ownership through L1. Units in meters are converted (on004473, on005415).

The implementer may choose: helper structure, how `talairach.xfm` is parsed (document the format you accept).
