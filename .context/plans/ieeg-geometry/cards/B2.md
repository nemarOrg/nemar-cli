## Goal

`ieeg_geometry.spm` maps contacts from a subject's native frame to SPM's template space by inverting an SPM deformation field the dataset ships, verifies the result, and produces a `space-IXI549Space` electrodes and coordsystem pair (on004457, sub-1 to sub-4).

## Context

- The plan's "Lead rulings" section (evidence grades, the sEEG plausibility rule, the c_ras choice, open references, one-hemisphere surfaces) overrides this card wherever they differ; use `Fit`'s evidence grade, never a raw threshold of your own.
- Read the plan, `census.md` (track B and the on004457 pitfalls), the L1 package.
- on004457's `derivatives/freesurfer/sub-N/y_*.nii` are 121x145x121x1x3 fields on SPM12's template grid (1.5 mm), intent deformation: each template voxel holds the native-space millimeter position it maps to (template to native). Mapping a native contact to the template therefore needs the inverse: find the template point whose field value equals the contact.
- Which electrodes file a field belongs to: sub-1's field is `y_sub-1_ses-mri01_T1w_acpc.nii` and matches the `ACPC` electrodes; sub-2 to sub-4's fields match the native (`Other`) electrodes; the census found sub-2's and sub-4's `space-ACPC` files do not match the shipped surfaces. Decide pairing by evidence (L1 fit of the electrodes to the subject surface the field's T1 belongs to, or the field's value range against the electrode extent), never by file name alone.
- Data: `https://data.nemar.org/on004457/<version>/<path>`; commit only a cropped field fixture (a sub-volume around the contacts) or a synthetic field.

## Scope

`scripts/ieeg_geometry/ieeg_geometry/spm.py`, its tests and fixtures; nothing else.

## Decided design

Settled:
- Inversion: KD-tree over the field's values for a starting voxel, then Newton iterations on the trilinearly interpolated field until the residual is below 0.05 mm (at most 20 iterations); a contact that does not converge, or lands outside the field's grid, is refused.
- The template affine comes from the field's NIfTI header; output is in millimeters of that grid's space, labeled `IXI549Space` (SPM12's default template space per BIDS).
- Verification with L1 against `MNI152NLin2009cAsym` using the sEEG rule; a failing subject is refused and reported, never written.
- Sidecars and ownership as in card C1; provenance names the field file, the inversion residuals (median and maximum) and that SPM spaces differ from MNI152NLin2009cAsym by a few millimeters.

The implementer may choose: the Jacobian computation (finite differences or analytic trilinear), fixture construction.

## Acceptance

- `uv run --project scripts/ieeg_geometry pytest -q` passes with tests that each fail without the code, including: a synthetic field built from a known affine plus a smooth warp is inverted to within 0.1 mm of the known answer for 50 random points; a point outside the grid is refused; the on004457 cropped fixture's contacts invert with a median residual under 0.05 mm and pass verification; the pairing logic picks the native file for an on004457 sub-2 style case.
- `python -m ieeg_geometry inspect` on the fixture shows pairing evidence, residuals and verification; quote it in the PR.
