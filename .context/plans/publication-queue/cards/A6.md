## Goal

A `.zip` file's entry names are screened, and small inner recordings in a format with a registered reader are screened through the registry.
Anything not read is reported under its inner format.
This affects 129 queued datasets.

## Context

- ZIP layout: the end of central directory record (with the ZIP64 locator and record), the central directory, and local file headers.
  The reference is PKWARE APPNOTE 6.3.10.
- Read the central directory through A1's `RangeSource` with Range reads, never the whole archive.
- Inner entries are scanned with `scanPaths` (prefixing the archive path) and, where a reader exists, with that reader over an in-memory `RangeSource`.

## Scope

A new `shared/readers/zip.ts`, its registration, `KIND_COPY` and `INCOMPLETE_COPY` entries, tests and fixtures.

## Decided design

Settled:
- **Read:** the last 64 KiB for the end record, then the central directory, capped at 4 MiB and 50,000 entries; past either cap the read is `partial` with reason `limit`.
- **Entry names** go through `scanPaths` as `<archive>!/<entry>`, so a path finding names the archive.
- **An inner entry is read** when its extension has a reader, its method is stored (0) or deflate (8), its uncompressed size is at most 64 MiB, and it is not encrypted.
  It is inflated with `DecompressionStream("deflate-raw")`.
  The inner reader's findings and coverage count toward the dataset.
  Nesting is followed one level only; a ZIP inside a ZIP is listed by name and counted unscreened.
- **An inner entry that is not read** is counted in `unscreened` as `zip:<ext>`.
  An encrypted entry is `partial` with reason `encrypted`.
- **The archive counts as fully read** only when every inner recording entry was read completely.

The implementer may choose: the internals.

## Acceptance

- **False-positive gate (owner rule).**
  Run the reader over at least 200 public files of its format.
  Get them from OpenNeuro's public bucket (`aws s3 cp --no-sign-request s3://openneuro.org/<ds>/...`, headers or metadata only where the format allows a ranged read) or from PhysioNet open data.
  Report the findings per kind per 100 files in the PR.
  - Identifier-severity findings must be 0, or each one is explained without its value.
  - Review-severity findings must be at most 5 per 100 files.
  - The script is `scripts/dev/reader-fp-measure.ts` (shared; create it if absent).
  - The downloaded files stay in the OS temp directory and are deleted afterwards.

- **Fixtures:** a real archive from a public dataset that ships zipped sourcedata, or one built from A2, A4 or A5 fixtures with `zip`, recorded as such.
  Also a ZIP64 archive built with Info-ZIP `zip -fz`, and an archive with an encrypted entry built with `zip -e` and a throwaway password recorded in the README.
  Also a crafted archive whose entry declares 1 KiB but inflates past the cap, which must stop at the cap (a zip bomb).
- `bun test test/readers-zip.test.ts test/identifier-fleet.test.ts` passes.
  - Inner EDF findings surface with the archive path.
  - An inner `.xdf` (no reader) is counted as `zip:.xdf`, and an inner EDF is read.
  - Encrypted gives `partial`; a truncated archive gives `partial`.
- Bytes read for an archive whose inner entries all lack readers are at most the central directory size plus 64 KiB.
- A revert check per new rule.
