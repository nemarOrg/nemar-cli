## Goal

When a publication request's license lineage cannot be confirmed, the depositor is told exactly what to send and where.
They send evidence from the original authors to support@nemar.org.
An administrator records that evidence against the request, linked to the support ticket, and the request moves on.
The workflow is named, audited and documented.

## Context

- **Owner decision, 2026-11-25:** "if it failed because of the license, the message is: the license does not agree; please provide supporting evidence or supporting documents from the original authors that they accept this specific license on the copy that we are publishing."
  The depositor sends the evidence to the support email, an administrator resolves it, and an email chain from the authors is acceptable evidence.
- **Where things live:**
  - The license-lineage outcomes and their copy are in `shared/license-lineage.ts` (`LINEAGE_COPY`; `needs_permission` at about line 209).
  - The review reason `license_lineage_unconfirmed` is in `shared/publication-review.ts` (about lines 805, 1669 and 1885); it is cleared today only by an administrator's override.
  - Support tickets are covered by ADR 0112 (`.context/decisions/0112-support-tickets.md`), and support@nemar.org is the inbound address.
  - `redistribution_prohibited` (PhysioNet credentialed or restricted) needs the source repository's own permission, so the depositor's authors alone cannot clear it.
- **ADR 0139** decision 7 and its amendments: these outcomes are action required and never denied automatically.

## Decided design

Settled:
- **Depositor message.** One copy entry per outcome, worded from the owner's sentence and printed wherever the outcome is shown (status, mail, CLI):
  - `needs_permission`, `share_alike_mismatch` and `source_is_publication`: "The license of the upstream data ((source id), (source license)) does not permit this copy under (target license). Please send evidence from the original authors that they accept this copy being published under (target license), for example an email from them, to support@nemar.org, quoting (dataset id). An administrator will record it and the review will continue."
  - `redistribution_prohibited`: the same request, naming the source repository's permission (for example PhysioNet's) instead of the authors'.
  - `unresolved`: "NEMAR could not read the upstream license; reply to support@nemar.org with a link to it, or wait for the next check."
  The placeholders are filled from the lineage result, never from free text.
- **Notice.** When a review stores `license_lineage_unconfirmed`, the depositor gets one mail with that copy, and it is recorded so that it is sent once per request and outcome.
  The mail goes through the existing dev mail gate.
  The publication status shows the same lines.
- **Recording.** `license_permissions` (`id`, `dataset_id`, `request_id`, `outcome`, `source_ids`, `ticket_id`, `evidence` (at most 1,000 characters, the administrator's own summary of what was received), `recorded_by`, `recorded_at`).
  - The command is a dry run by default, showing the request, its outcome and its sources; `--apply` records.
  - The ticket must exist (ADR 0112).
  - An audit row `license_permission_recorded` is written in the same batch.
  - The evidence text is never sent to a model and never shown to anyone except administrators.
- **Effect.** A recorded permission whose `source_ids` cover every source behind the request's lineage outcome clears `license_lineage_unconfirmed` for that request and every later request of the same dataset with the same sources.
  The review then derives its verdict without that reason.
  A permission never clears any other reason and never approves.
  The admin show and the mail print "License permission recorded on (date) by (administrator), ticket (ticket)".
- **ADR 0139 amendment** (append, dated 2026-11-25): "A license lineage that needs permission is cleared only by an administrator recording the evidence received, linked to a support ticket. The depositor is told what to send and where. The permission covers the named sources, and later requests with the same sources. It clears that reason alone."

The implementer may choose: the table's index layout, and the exact layout of the notice mail.

## Acceptance

- `bun test` passes on the new tests, which show:
  - each outcome's message, with no free text interpolated;
  - the notice sent exactly once per request and outcome, and fenced in dev;
  - the dry run records nothing, and `--apply` records with its audit row;
  - an unknown ticket is refused;
  - a recorded permission clears the reason for that request and for a later request with the same sources, and not for one with a different source;
  - a permission never clears another reason;
  - the evidence text never appears in the depositor's status or in model inputs.
- Mutation checks: widen the source match to any source, and let a permission clear `question_failed`. Both must fail a test.
