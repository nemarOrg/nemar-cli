ADR 0121, numbered decisions: D1 (executor and accounting class; the two selector rules; a Tapis job is always `su`), D2 (no new state; `runner_claimed_at`; the edges used; `runner_lost` and its four details; `submitted` and `queued` unused by `runner/local`, `runner/slurm` decided later), D3 (pull only, no inbound port, the User-Agent), D4 (protocol `nemar.runner.v1`; claim returns a list; leases; deadline; acceptance by the current lease; at least once execution, at most once acceptance), D5 (owner-only registration; revoke ends the runner's work in one batch; the three cascade callers; why a key revocation is not one), D6 (no ledger entry and a zero-charge settled stamp for `none`; runner-reported usage; the runner guard; partition as a sizing label; `su` runner jobs refused until built), D7 (`COMPUTE_RUNNERS_ENABLED`), D14's rule (derivatives never go into the serving copy or the dataset repository), every Tapis admission path (including #2121 P3's when it lands) selects `executor = 'tapis'`, and "a result is reproducible from image digest, launch specification and input snapshot with no runner and no Tapis".
