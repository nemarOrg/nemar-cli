## Goal

One real, public EEG dataset with a verified Zarr serving copy and two to six EEG recordings is chosen, with the evidence that it meets every criterion; the recipe will process all of its EEG recordings.

## Context

- Production public reads only. Run the CLI as `env -u TEST_API_URL NEMAR_CONFIG_DIR=<empty temp dir> NEMAR_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1 bun run src/index.ts ...` (the session shell may point the CLI at the test API).
- The index contract is `shared/zarr-index.schema.json`, format version 3, at `https://zarr.nemar.org/<id>/zarr/index.json`; send `-A nemar-demo-probe/0` (ADR 0049 recorded a 403 for `Python-urllib`).
- Pipeline eligibility (ADR 0118, amendment of 2026-11-05, item 5): active, public, `anonymous = 0`, first published, not withdrawn, a version with a data-plane tag, `eeg` as a modality token, a real `nm` or `on` id outside `nm099900`-`nm099999`.
- Why whole datasets of two to six recordings: a run over a chosen subset would stamp the dataset version as done; a store selector waits for step Q0's stamp scope.

## Scope

No repository file changes. Evidence goes to `<operator scratch>/dm02/`.

## Decided design

Settled: candidates from `env -u TEST_API_URL bun run src/index.ts dataset list --modality eeg --has-zarr-verified --limit 200 --json`.
Exclude `nm000103`-`nm000107` and every reserved-band id.
Keep a dataset only if: its `index.json` lists between 2 and 6 stores whose group is EEG; every EEG store has at most 128 channels and channels times samples at most 2 x 10^8 (the reader's 2 GiB float64 bound with a margin); `GET https://api.nemar.org/datasets/<id>` shows `visibility` public and `anonymous` false; its version has a data-plane tag; and the index's `source_commit` equals the commit of that tag (read from the data plane's version listing; record both).
Choose the one with the smallest total level-0 samples times channels; record the runner-up.
The implementer may choose: the tie-break (lowest id).

## Acceptance

- The report names the dataset id, its version tag, the tag's commit, the index's `source_commit`, the index SHA-256 (`curl -sS -A nemar-demo-probe/0 https://zarr.nemar.org/<id>/zarr/index.json | shasum -a 256`), and for every EEG store: path, channels, sampling rate, samples.
- The report quotes `visibility`, `anonymous`, `first_published_at` and the modality list from `datasets/<id>`.

## Gates
