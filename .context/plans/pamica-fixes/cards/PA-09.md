## Goal

eegprep's E1 test compares `pop_runica(icatype='pamica')` with pAMICA's released binary started from the same seed and fails, instead of passing or failing by chance, when they disagree beyond the tier pAMICA attained.

## Context

Settled intent (the lead completes the rest at CP-B):
- Today (owner decision, 2026-11-14) E1 in eegprep PR #617 is informational, because it compares pamica with EEGLAB's shipped binary, which seeds itself from the clock; a run passed once (minimum 0.9966) and failed on a rerun (minimum 0.5339).
- The gate compares, on `sample_data/eeglab_data.set`, `pop_runica(EEG, icatype='pamica', seed=s)` with `pamica.AMICANative(start="pamica", seed=s, threads=1, **pamica.presets.RUNAMICA15)` running the release's binary (resolved and SHA-checked by pamica's resolver), with the same `block_size` and `max_iter`.
- The threshold is the one `cp-a-note.md` part 5 names: Tier C if PA-03 attained it, else the distributional rule (Amari to the binary's same-start fit at most the largest among five binary seeds' pairs).
- The comparison against EEGLAB's own binary stays, labeled informational.
- eegprep's pin becomes the pamica release from PA-10; `eegprep`'s E1 Amari divides by 2n, pAMICA's by 2n(n-1): the gate uses pAMICA's `amari_distance` and says so.
