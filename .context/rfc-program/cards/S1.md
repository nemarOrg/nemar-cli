## Goal

## Decided design

Settled: measure, in this order:

1. EDF+D and BDF+D files with real gaps (from the earlier scan, plus any datasets it could not list).
2. BrainVision recordings with more than one `New Segment` marker (from the `.vmrk` files, Range reads only).
3. FIF recordings with acquisition skips (from the FIF tag directory, Range reads only); if this needs a full download per file, measure a fixed-seed sample of 50 and say so.
4. For each class, the number of stores (recordings) and datasets affected.
5. Conversion throughput: from the public API's `zarr_converted_at` across datasets over the last 30 days, the stores converted per day (median and 90th percentile), and the projected days to re-convert all 231,259 stores and the affected subset first.

The implementer may choose: the script language and the sample seed.
