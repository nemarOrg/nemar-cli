## Decided design

Settled:
Asides become blockquotes that keep their label (for example `> **Caution.**`);
site-relative links become absolute `https://docs.nemar.org/...` links;
the Starlight front matter is removed and the title becomes the first heading;
everything else is unchanged, including all 33 open questions, the root attribute table, the "Time axis" paragraph and the dequantization section.
`tools/check-moved-text.ts` compares the word sequences of the source and the moved file after stripping Markdown markup and link targets, and prints the count of differing words.
