## Decided design

Settled (owner decisions of 2026-11-20 and the lead's reading of D2):

- The specification repository nemarOrg/biosig-zarr exists now, not at beta (owner decision 3); the beta criteria themselves are unchanged.
- `.bzx` packing rules are NEMAR's own; OME-Zarr RFC-9 is prior art and later changes to RFC-9 are not followed (owner decision 5).
- Store metadata is never patched in place (ADR 0128 stands on this, and D2 forbids a metadata-only upgrade), so the in-place patch step of issue 2217 is withdrawn: the rename of the format tag from `biosigio-zarr` to `biosig-zarr` lands with the version 3 writer, through re-conversion, and the rename "with format_version unchanged" is superseded.
- Specification decisions are recorded in nemarOrg/biosig-zarr's `.context/decisions/`; NEMAR profile and rollout decisions stay in nemar-cli; each links the RFC that produced it.
- Everything else in ADR 0128 stands, including that the serving copy is not an NWB container.
