## Decided design

Settled:
The page keeps its address and its title, so existing links keep working.
Its body is at most 15 lines: what biosig-zarr is in two sentences; a link to `https://github.com/nemarOrg/biosig-zarr/blob/2.0.0/spec/index.md` (the released text) and to the repository; a link to the RFC index in nemarOrg/rfcs; and one sentence that the specification is licensed in that repository.
No part of the specification text stays on the page.
The "Pre-beta" sidebar badge stays only if `astro.config.mjs` already has it.
The implementer may choose: the wording of the two sentences.
