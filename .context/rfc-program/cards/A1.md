## Decided design

Settled:
Status `accepted`, Date 2026-11-20 or the day of the PR, Owner Seyed Yahya Shirazi.
The Decision states: one public repository, nemarOrg/rfcs, is the record and the one number space (`NEMAR RFC 0001`);
every comment thread, organization or dataset scope, is a Discussion in nemarOrg/rfcs;
the window is at most 21 calendar days in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), with one extension of at most 14 days granted before the close with a stated reason;
an accepted RFC produces an ADR whose header line reads `Decided through NEMAR RFC NNNN (link).`, and the ADR wins over the RFC where they disagree;
plus the answers to Q1 to Q7 as the manager pastes them;
at most one RFC is open for comment at a time (owner decision, 2026-11-20);
specification decisions are recorded in the specification's own repository and NEMAR profile and rollout decisions in nemar-cli, each linking its RFC.
Consequences says the process is provisional and is reviewed after its first two RFCs, and names what an RFC is not for (a privacy correction under ADR 0085, a security fix, an internal architecture choice, an ordinary dataset pull request).
Alternatives considered: organization Discussions only; per-dataset Discussions on nemarDatasets repositories (rejected by the owner on 2026-11-20: no App permission change, one place to comment); open-ended windows like BIDS Extension Proposals (BEPs).
The number is the next one unused on `origin/dev`, on `origin/feature/rfc-program`, and in open PRs (`gh pr list -R nemarOrg/nemar-cli --state open --search ADR`).
The implementer may choose: the wording, within two screens.
