## Decided design

Settled, from the owner decisions of 2026-11-20:

- Front matter: `number: 1`, `scope: org`, `window_days: 21`, `discussion`, `opened_at` and decision fields null, shepherd the owner.
- One RFC for the whole format; `.bzx` is its single-file form.
- A section "Why the format and the package have separate names": two layers, the store and the packing of one store into one file.
  It lists the package-specific items for comment: the commenters differ (people who use electroencephalography (EEG) and magnetoencephalography (MEG) data, and people who build Zarr tools); the names `.bzx` and `biosig-zarr` (one listing ties `.bzx` to other software, and BioSig is a long-standing library); what a `.bzx` snapshot of a BIDS dataset changes around it (rate-dependent sidecar fields rewritten, `events.parquet` never packed, the light events group); and whether a fix to the packing rules raises `format_version`, with the recommendation that one `format_version` covers both.
- A section "Relation to OME-Zarr RFC-9": cited as prior art, not a dependency.
  NEMAR's packing rules are its own and do not follow later changes to RFC-9, and the RFC presents that independence as an advantage (RFC-9 was at stage R4, not accepted, when checked on 2026-11-20).
  A table compares each RFC-9 rule with NEMAR's: adopted, strengthened, changed, or not adopted.
- A section "Why not NWB on Zarr", fair and transparent in tone:
  it names `hdmf-zarr` and links `https://nwb.org/tools/core/hdmf-zarr/`, and says plainly that it is a great tool;
  then it gives the reasons it does not fit NEMAR's case: NEMAR serves human, low-frequency biosignals organized around signal files that follow BIDS principles, where a data file sits beside its metadata sidecars, not a container that holds the metadata;
  grounded in ADR 0128's three reasons (separate scope, technical incompatibility, an unsettled NWB Zarr specification), its stateless-export position, and its revisit conditions, each cited by content;
  and it is open for comment.
- **Time model and resolution: the owner's rulings D1 to D12 of 2026-11-20** (board log; design in `research/irregular-and-resolution-design.md`), proposed as `format_version` 3.
  The Problem section states that European Data Format (EDF) cannot represent irregular or sparse series and that the current draft cannot either (quote the "Time axis" paragraph and open question 9 with B2's line numbers), and that the root `dtype` and per-channel `scale` and `offset` record storage but not the source recording's resolution.
  It cites the confirmed EDF+D case (neuromechanist/biosigio#180, mne-tools/mne-python#13429) as a present-day failure, not a hypothetical.
  The Proposal carries D1 to D12: the four reader-safety layers; group kinds `uniform` and `timestamped`; `valid_intervals` with `source_time` null allowed; a reserved `missing_value` per data type; jittered streams kept as measured by default; `int32` and `float64` per group; bit-exact copies; the per-channel `derivation` list; the source fields `source_sample_type`, `source_adc_bits` (file-level default, channel override), `source_step`, `source_offset`, `source_digital_range` (per channel); `onset_source_basis`; the ADR 0123 digest v2; epoched sources refused in NEMAR's profile.
  It says plainly that version 3 is not only forward-looking: gaps (EDF+D, FIF skips, BrainVision segments) and resolution apply to data NEMAR already holds, while the `timestamped` kind gives a home to data that BIDS does not carry yet.
  The rejected alternatives from the design are summarized, and the questions for commenters include its community questions on time and resolution.
  Cite the BIDS threads listed in plan section 7 and BIDS issue 1771's 2024-04-22 thread (where EDF+D was raised for BEP047, audio and video) by link with "checked 2026-11-20".
- **A section near the end, "History of the format"** (owner request, 2026-11-20): so readers see that version 3 is a maturing of a format NEMAR has served for a while, now becoming its own specification.
  A short dated table of each `format_version` (1, 2, the proposed 3): when it started, what it changed, and the records behind it (ADRs and pull requests, cited by content), with dates taken from git history and the ADRs, not estimated.
  Current scale: the number of Zarr stores NEMAR serves (the owner states more than 230,000), measured read-only at writing time with the command and date quoted; if the measurement cannot be made, write "more than 230,000 (owner-reported, 2026-11-20)" and say it is unmeasured.
  Also, if measurable read-only, how many stores are at each `format_version` and how many datasets they cover.
- A section "Decided, and out of scope for comment", each item citing its record: not an NWB container; one package holds one recording; entries stored without compression; written once; packages built on the researcher's machine; the license model.
- Questions for commenters: numbered, answerable, grouped (format, package, names, irregular and sparse series, resolution, why not NWB).
- "How the decision will be made": criteria written before comments open.
- A note that RFC-0001 proposes normative changes to the `2.0.0-draft.1` text, which the next draft tag would carry if accepted.

The implementer may choose: wording, section order (the history section stays near the end), and how to present the design's JSON examples.
