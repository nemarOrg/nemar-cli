## Decided design

Settled (Q9, Q34):

- The format-level conformance vectors, their manifest and their goldens (biosig-zarr `schemas/` and `conformance/`) are canonical in nemarOrg/biosig-zarr; a change to them is made there, through that repository's process, and only there.
- nemar-cli vendors them byte for byte into `shared/contract/zarr-conformance/biosig-zarr/`, pinned to a biosig-zarr release tag and to the SHA-256 of `conformance/manifest.json`, with a drift check that fails on a copy edited in place; the other repositories that hold a reader do the same.
- The NEMAR-profile cases (the `nemar` provenance block, NEMAR's other root additions, and the store content digest of ADR 0123 decision 4 with its vectors) stay canonical in nemar-cli `shared/contract/zarr-conformance/`, as ADR 0124 decision 3 says.
- Everything else in ADR 0124 stands, decisions 1, 2 and 4 to 7 included.
- Nothing is vendored by this ADR; the first change that vendors the vectors follows it.
