Its goal is to tell versions made by the dataset's source (its authors, or upstream for a mirror) apart from additions by NEMAR or others,
and to fix the legibility problem that every mirror re-pull is a major version (ADR 0006).
It presents the problem and the options with their measured costs:
an origin recorded for each version with the upstream tag shown;
revisiting the rule that a re-pull is a major version;
and `+N` or another version grammar (research note, sections B3 to B5 and B9).
Grouped additions (a Hierarchical Event Descriptors (HED) campaign, derivatives) are a question for commenters, not a settled rule.
It is never dispatched before the lead's card PR merges.
