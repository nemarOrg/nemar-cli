## Decided design

Settled: front matter `title: Requests for Comment` and a one-line `description`;
sections in this order: what an RFC is for and not for; the two scopes (organization, one dataset) and that every thread is in nemarOrg/rfcs; the states; the clock (21 days at most, one extension of at most 14 days); how to comment, with or without a GitHub account; how a decision is recorded (the ADR link); one sentence on how an RFC differs from a BIDS Extension Proposal (bounded in time, ends in a recorded decision); a link to the index.
